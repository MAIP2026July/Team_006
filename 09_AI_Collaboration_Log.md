## AI Collaboration Log

### Tools Used
- Gemini / Antigravity AI Assistant

### Where AI Helped
- **Drafting:** Structuring the PRD, Opportunity Matrix, and Executive Summary to align perfectly with the MBA rubric constraints.
- **Metrics:** Defining specific, measurable business metrics (Free-to-Paid conversion rate) and technical metrics (STT Word Error Rate).
- **Risk Review:** Identifying blind spots in data privacy (e.g., LLM providers using resumes for training data) and bias (accent recognition in the mock interview).

### Example Prompts or Workflows
- *Prompt 1:* "Draft a PRD based on a question generation tool."
- *Prompt 2:* "We have one more key feature... AI mock interview, where we use voice to text... Can we also include that now?"
- *Prompt 3:* "I guess we are missing some core idea, here preplens ai is for candidates mainly not for recruiters. Rewrite the product strategy."

### Where AI Was Weak or Wrong
- **Weak prioritization:** Initially, the AI suggested building a B2B product targeting HR and Hiring Managers (a common default assumption for interview tools).
- **Unrealistic recommendations:** The AI initially placed the Voice Mock Interview in a distant "Phase 4" roadmap, underestimating its core value to the B2C pitch.

### Human Corrections
- **What we changed:** We completely pivoted the Target Persona from "Hiring Manager" to "Job Seeker / Candidate" based on the actual PrepLens presentation deck. We also moved the Voice Mock Interview into the core MVP value proposition.
- **Why we changed it:** The business model relies on B2C micro-transactions (₹40 unlocks) rather than enterprise B2B SaaS subscriptions.
- **What judgment was applied:** Product judgment was used to recognize that a B2C model requires an immediate "wow" factor (the Voice Mock Interview) to drive impulse conversions, whereas a B2B tool would prioritize ATS integrations.

### Final Reflection
- **Where AI accelerated PM work:** The AI drastically reduced the time spent formatting tables, drafting boilerplate structure, and brainstorming baseline metrics. It acted as an excellent sounding board for risk analysis.
- **Where AI could not replace PM reasoning:** The AI could not define the core business model or target user without explicit human guidance. It required human intervention to recognize the B2C monetization strategy and pivot the entire product strategy to align with the actual PrepLens slide deck. AI can format a strategy, but the human must define it.
