## Functional Prototype / MVP

### Prototype Type
Live Web Application (B2C SaaS)

### What It Demonstrates
The current PrepLens prototype demonstrates the core product value loop:
1. **Contextual Matching:** Uses candidate context such as a resume, Job Description, career stage, and role information to produce more relevant preparation.
2. **Personalized Preparation:** Generates tailored interview questions and paid preparation content instead of a static question bank.
3. **Monetization Workflow:** Supports a free preview followed by one-time paid paths: **Basic ₹30, Pro ₹60, and AI Mock Interview ₹100**.
4. **AI Mock Interview:** Demonstrates voice/answer practice and feedback as part of the candidate preparation journey.

### AI Role
AI creates value where the workflow requires contextual synthesis rather than deterministic matching: it interprets unstructured candidate/job information, generates role-specific questions and answer scaffolding, and supports adaptive interview practice. Deterministic software remains responsible for validation, payment state, session limits, and measurable speech statistics where applicable.

### What It Does Not Yet Prove
- **Production-Scale Voice Economics:** The prototype demonstrates the AI mock-interview experience, but it does not yet prove speech accuracy, concurrency, latency, and contribution margin at large production scale.
- **Generalization Across All Domains:** Current product evidence is strongest for the intended early user segments and must be evaluated before expanding broadly across non-technical domains.
- **Measured Commercial Performance:** Pricing and conversion logic are live product hypotheses; conversion, repeat purchase, CAC, LTV, and contribution margin require pilot data before they can be treated as validated business results.

### Product Evolution from Topic Approval
The original topic-approval hypothesis focused on **hiring managers, recruiters, and educators** as the user. During product development, the problem was reframed around the **candidate preparation journey**, producing the current B2C product. This change is intentionally retained as evidence of product learning rather than rewriting the historical approval artifact.
